Rong Li       李 蓉  

I am a Ph.D. candidate in AI Thrust at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou campus) , where I am supervised by Prof. Junwei Liang and co-supervised by Prof. Yingcong Chen. I obtained my Bachelor's and Master's degrees from South China University of Science and Technology.

My research focuses on Spatial Intelligence, and Embodied AI. I study how multimodal models and embodied agents perceive, reason about, and interact with their environments.

I am always open to research discussions and collaborations.
